## Design Context

### Users
Mixed-level builders at Coffee & Code Philly workshops (~20 min talk + hands-on lab). Many will not read dense slides; they need scannable visuals while the speaker narrates. Projected 16:9 (1280×720).

### Brand Personality
Confident, technical, approachable — “ops workshop,” not SaaS brochure. Three words: **clear, industrial, teachable**.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Light industrial / blueprint-paper workshop look
- Cyan (`#0088B8`) as the only decorative accent + semantic warn/critical/success
- Typography: Outfit (display) + IBM Plex Sans/Mono (body/terminal)
- Anti-references: purple SaaS gradients, equal card grids, soft glow/glass, Inter-default AI decks, dark neon CNCF cosplay
- Atmosphere: subtle cyan grid + left cyan spine rule; flat panels with inset accent, not rounded white cards

### Design Principles
1. **One idea per slide** — headline + dominant visual + at most one takeaway rule
2. **Title owns the cover** — Meetup photo is secondary; Game Plan mark sits under role only
3. **Semantic color only** — cyan/warn/critical/success mean something; no purple washes
4. **First paint is complete** — never leave the first beat behind a click
5. **Terminal honesty** — commands and YAML live in dark panels with real contrast
